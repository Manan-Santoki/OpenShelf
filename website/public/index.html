How it works

One gesture, three wheels.

Press on a file in Finder, on the Desktop or in any app that lets you drag files. While you're still dragging, tap a key. The wheel blooms under the pointer and stays open until you drop. Results land next to the original, and nothing is ever overwritten.

⇧

Convert

Only the formats that make sense for what you're dragging. JPG to HEIC, PDF to Word, video to GIF, folder to ZIP.

⇧⌥

Tools

Crop, resize, remove backgrounds, annotate, trim, merge, split PDFs. Each tool opens a small focused editor and saves a copy.

⌃

Actions

Put it on a floating shelf, AirDrop it, copy it, move it, copy its path or open Terminal in its folder.

Feedback right where you're looking.

  • The label above the wheel shows the file size, then exactly what the hovered wedge will do.
  • A small card by the pointer shows progress for long jobs. Click × to cancel.
  • No drag needed: select files in Finder and press ⌃⌥Space. Arrow keys choose, Tab switches wheels, Return runs it.
  • Keyboard mid-drag too: open a wheel, let go of the key, then use the arrow keys and Return. Esc cancels.
  • Your keys: change the key for each wheel in Settings.

Tools

Small editors for quick jobs.

Each tool opens a focused window that zooms out of where you dropped the file. Save writes a copy beside the original (Photo Cropped.jpg), so you can drag the result straight back into another wheel.

A filmstrip for video, a waveform for audio. Drag the handles to scrub, press Space to hear the selection, then save just that part.

Shelves

A place to put files for a minute.

Drop files on the Shelf wedge and they wait in a small floating window, so you can gather them from anywhere and drag them out together later.

  • Collapsed stack or full grid and list. Select like Finder, preview with Space.
  • Dock to a screen edge. It tucks into a slim tab and slides back out when you hover or drop on it.
  • Comes back after a restart, even if the files moved.
  • Never touches your files. Closing a shelf just lets go of them.

Install

Ready in a minute.

  1. Download the DMG and open it.
  2. Drag OpenShelf onto the Applications folder, then open it. It lives in your menu bar.
  3. First launch only: macOS may say it can't verify the app. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. On macOS 14, right-click the app and choose Open instead.
  4. Try it: drag any file, tap ⇧, and drop on a wedge.

Prefer Terminal?

After moving the app to Applications, this clears the first-launch prompt:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/OpenShelf.app

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Later versions arrive through the app's built-in updater.
